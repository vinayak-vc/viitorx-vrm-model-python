================================================================================================================
 (8b) MECHANISM -- what physically destroys the hip sign
================================================================================================================
 (i) DEPTH IS QUANTISED TO INTEGER DISPARITY.  z = f*B/d, and the levels actually used
     near the hips are: 1179, 1248, 1326, 1415, 1516, 1632, 1768 mm
     Fitting z*d over those levels gives f*B = 21216 mm.px, constant to <1%.
     At the subject's 1326 mm the disparity is d=16, and the neighbouring rungs are
     1248 mm and 1414 mm -- so ONE STEP is 78-88 mm, mean 83 mm.

 (ii) GEOMETRY.  Measured hip separation = 130 mm, so a turn of theta puts the hips
      dz = 130 * sin(theta) mm apart:
        theta = 15 deg -> true dz =   33.7 mm = 0.41 disparity steps
        theta = 30 deg -> true dz =   65.2 mm = 0.78 disparity steps
        theta = 45 deg -> true dz =   92.2 mm = 1.11 disparity steps
        theta = 60 deg -> true dz =  112.9 mm = 1.36 disparity steps
        theta = 90 deg -> true dz =  130.4 mm = 1.57 disparity steps
      A 45 deg turn is worth 1.1 steps. The sign is a comparison of two numbers that
      each move in 83 mm jumps -- so both hips land on the SAME rung most of the time.

 (iii) AT +-90 THE FAR HIP IS OCCLUDED BY THE NEAR ONE.
 heading         n   uSpan px   |dz| p50      dz sd   measured   conf p50
 0            1000       43.9        0.0       22.8    100.00%      0.655
 +45           380       30.1       78.0      102.6    100.00%      0.621
 -45           371       30.8        0.0       44.0    100.00%      0.635
 +90           275       14.6        0.0        0.0    100.00%      0.491
 -90           294       10.6        0.0       19.0    100.00%      0.461
     At +-90 the two hips project close together and the far one is behind the near one,
     so both 5x5 depth windows land on the SAME body surface: dz collapses to 0 with
     zero spread. The pose that most needs a sign is the pose that cannot produce one.
