  MECHANISM, measured on the same frames:
    raw shoulder dz            = -101 mm
    one disparity step at 1.33 m = 89 mm
  The two shoulders land on ADJACENT disparity rungs -- the smallest non-zero difference the
  sensor can express -- and that single step becomes ~50 deg of commanded torso yaw.
  It is not noise: it is a stable, confident, wrong measurement, and no filter can remove it.

  THIS IS NOT A V6 DEFECT. -49.6 deg is INSIDE the +-60 envelope, so the guard correctly passes
  it through as Valid. V6 governs the WRAP and the RANGE; it does not touch how yaw is MEASURED.
  This is the F-09 under-resolution root cause (ADR-038), listed as V6 limitation section 10.4.

  It also explains the V6 replay oddity: yaw3D read -0.1 deg for a real 58.6 deg turn on h_m45.
  The magnitude is largely decoupled from the truth in BOTH directions.
